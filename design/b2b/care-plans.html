FAQ

Questions.

Something else? Email hello@arcnest.dev

Do you look after sites you didn't build?

Yes, if they run on WordPress. We check the site first, fix anything urgent, then add it to a plan.

What counts as a content change?

Updating text and images, adding a team member, a case study or a new page from your existing blocks. New designs or features are quoted separately.

Is hosting included?

No. Your site stays on hosting in your name, so you're never tied to us. We can recommend and set up a provider if you need one.

What happens if my site goes down?

Monitoring alerts us, and we start working on it. If something breaks after an update, we restore from backup and fix it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with a month's notice. You keep everything, and we hand over backups and notes so the next person can carry on.
