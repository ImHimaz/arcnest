FAQ

Questions.

Something else? Email hello@arcnest.dev

Do I have to pay for any licences?

Not for a page builder. Our theme and blocks come with your site, with no yearly fee. If your site needs a paid plugin, such as a booking system, we tell you its cost before we start.

Can my team edit the site after launch?

Yes. You edit in WordPress's own editor, with blocks made for your content, so updates are quick and hard to break.

Will my site look the same after a rebuild?

Yes. We match your current design closely, or improve it where it helps. The difference is under the hood: cleaner code and much faster pages.

What happens if I stop the care plan?

Nothing breaks. Your site doesn't depend on us or any subscription. You can manage it yourself or hand it to any WordPress developer.

You're in Sri Lanka. How does that work?

We overlap with UK and Australian working hours and the US morning, reply within one working day, and send weekly written updates.
