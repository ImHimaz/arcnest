FAQ

Questions.

Something else? Email hello@arcnest.dev

Will my site look the same?

Yes. We match your current design closely. If there are things you've wanted to improve, we can do that at the same time; it's your choice, agreed before we start.

Will we lose our Google rankings?

We keep your addresses, page titles and descriptions, and add redirects wherever anything moves. A faster site usually helps rankings rather than hurting them.

Will the site go offline?

No. We build on a staging address and switch over in minutes, at a quiet time for your business.

Which builders do you rebuild from?

Elementor, Divi, WPBakery, Beaver Builder and most theme builders. If your site is on WordPress, we can rebuild it.

What happens to the builder licence?

Once the new site is live, you no longer need it. Cancel the renewal and keep the money.
