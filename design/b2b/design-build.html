FAQ

Questions.

Something else? Email hello@arcnest.dev

Do you write the words for the site?

We plan the structure and key messages of each page with you, so the words and the design work together. Tell us at the start if you'd also like the full copy written, and we'll include it in the plan.

Can you work with our existing brand?

Yes. We design within your logo, colours and fonts. If your brand needs a refresh, we'll tell you where and keep it consistent across the site.

Where will our site be hosted?

On hosting in your name. We recommend a provider that suits your traffic and budget, set it up with you, and you keep full control.

How long does it take?

Most business websites take six to eight weeks. Your fixed quote includes a timeline, and you'll get a written update every week.

What happens after launch?

Your team can edit the site straight away. If you'd like us to keep it updated, backed up and fast, add a care plan. It's optional, and the site never depends on it.
